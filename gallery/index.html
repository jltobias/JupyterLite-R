Data, accessibility, and reproducibility

Plots use numerical CSV exports from the R chapters. Use the camera slider as a keyboard alternative to dragging a 3D view. The full data and numerical summaries are available in the notebooks and CSV links. WebGL and CDN access are needed for the interactive views; the Book contains static figures. Changing a notebook does not change this saved gallery.

Regenerate with Rscript tools/export_gallery.R. The globe has no coastlines: its surface and graticule are generated mathematical geometry. Local terrain coordinates and elevations use metres; the sphere uses unit vectors. No data is sent to a plotting server.
